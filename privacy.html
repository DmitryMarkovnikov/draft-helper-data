draft-helper privacy policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

What the app stores

Your chosen roles, your champion pools, your language, the champions you marked as "this matchup is fine for me", and the last opponents you selected. All of this stays on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.

What the app downloads

Statistical data packages from this site. Downloading a package sends the usual HTTP request to GitHub Pages, which is operated by GitHub under its own privacy statement. The app sends no identifiers of its own.

Analytics

The current builds log usage events (app opened, role chosen, ranking shown, build opened) locally for debugging only; nothing leaves the device. If aggregated, anonymous usage analytics are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the events will never contain your pool, your device identifiers or any Riot account information.

Riot Games data

The app never asks for your Riot account, never reads your match history and never shows information about other players. The statistics it uses are aggregates over public ranked matches with no player identifiers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.
